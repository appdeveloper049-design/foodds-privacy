Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

FoOdds ("we", "our", or "the app") is a food decision app that helps you choose what to eat by spinning a wheel. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app handles, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Data We Collect

FoOdds is a local-first app. No account is required, and we (the developer) do not receive or have access to any information you enter into the app. Everything you enter stays on your device:

  • Your display name (optional, used only for the greeting on the Home screen)
  • Your saved foods (food names, plus how many times and when you last picked each one, which the wheel uses to vary its picks)
  • Quick Add options you type in for a single spin (these are not saved)
  • App settings (theme and whether dinner reminders are on)

We do not collect email addresses, passwords, contacts, photos, or your location. We do not run analytics or collect data in the background.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

All data you enter is stored locally on your device in an on-device database. Nothing you enter is uploaded to any server or cloud storage. If you delete the app, all of your FoOdds data is permanently deleted from your device.

3. Advertising

FoOdds is free to use and is supported by advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the following from your device:

  • Device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
  • Approximate location (from your IP address), device information, and advertising interaction data

On iOS, you will be asked via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt whether you allow personalised ads. If you decline, AdMob will show non-personalised ads only. In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland you will also be asked for ad consent, which you can change any time via the privacy icon on the Home screen. Your choices do not affect app functionality, and the foods and names you enter are never shared with AdMob.

For more information on how Google uses data in advertising, visit: Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Fonts

The first time you open FoOdds, the app downloads its fonts from Google Fonts. As with any download, Google's servers receive your device's IP address to deliver the font files. No information you enter into the app is sent. See Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party. The only third-party services the app uses are Google AdMob (Section 3) and Google Fonts (Section 4), as described above.

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, FoOdds sends an occasional dinner-time reminder (every few days) to help you decide what to eat. These reminders are scheduled locally on your device and do not involve sending any data to a server. You can turn them off at any time with the bell icon on the Home screen, or in your device settings.

7. Data Deletion

Because your data is stored only on your device, you are always in control of it. You can delete saved foods in the My Foods tab, change your display name at any time, or remove all FoOdds data by uninstalling the app.

For data collected by Google AdMob, you can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device's privacy settings.

8. Children's Privacy

FoOdds is a general audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
